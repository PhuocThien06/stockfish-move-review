Opening-position index and opening guide sourced from lichess-org/chess-openings (a.tsv to e.tsv).
Source: https://github.com/lichess-org/chess-openings
License: CC0 1.0 / public domain, as stated by the source project.
The source/*.tsv files are pinned to commit 48a8c0e733facfdc9a3723fe882e51ec28014074.
Each 64-bit value is a Polyglot Zobrist hash of a position along a listed
opening line. book_positions.bin is used to label conventional book moves.
The guide reads source/*.tsv to show opening names and possible continuations.
When connected, anonymous position counts come from https://explorer.lichess.org/masters
(Lichess Opening Explorer); no username or full PGN is sent.
